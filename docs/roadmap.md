# Roadmap

Rough priorities. Nothing here is a promise; open an issue to discuss or to pick something up.

## Next

- **Test background removal on real photos** and tune edge quality (hair, fine detail).
- **Close the open questions** in section 5 of [resize-canvas-spec.md](resize-canvas-spec.md),
  then add the remaining resampling modes: Fant, Bilinear (Low Quality), Adaptive (Sharp).
- **DPI and print size** in the Resize and Canvas Size dialogs (pixels, inches, centimeters).
- **Magic Wand** selection with tolerance and flood vs. global mode, as a manual way to remove
  or refine backgrounds.

## Later

- Crop, rotate and flip.
- Selection tools and a clipboard (copy and paste images).
- Layers.
- Basic adjustments (brightness and contrast, levels) and a few effects.
- App icon, Developer ID signing, notarization and downloadable releases.
- Localization.

## Not planned for v1

Plugins, a full brush engine, text tools, animation.
