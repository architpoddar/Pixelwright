# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First version.

- Resize: by percentage or absolute pixels, aspect-ratio lock, print size and resolution
  (inches or centimeters, saved with the file), gamma-correct option, and eight resampling
  methods: Bicubic, Bicubic (Smooth), Bilinear, Bilinear (Low Quality), Adaptive (Sharp),
  Lanczos, Fant and Nearest Neighbor.
- Canvas Size: 3x3 anchor, fill with transparent, white, black or a custom color.
- Remove Background: one click, using Apple's on-device Vision framework.
- Magic Wand: tolerance, flood or global mode, add and subtract, invert, expand and contract,
  delete selected pixels.
- Open and save PNG, JPEG, TIFF and HEIC. Undo and redo. Zoom and drag-and-drop.
